About me

Hi! I'm Noor Fatin Athirah Mohd Lazi, an IT professional based in Dengkil, Selangor.

I'm results-driven, with hands-on experience in IT operations, business analysis, software documentation and System Integration Testing (SIT/UAT). I like sitting between the business and the developers, turning what people need into clear requirements and making sure what ships actually works.

My journey started with a Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons.) at UiTM Shah Alam, followed by a web development internship, protégé and business analyst roles, and now IT operations at Toyyibpay, where I work on digital projects like ToyyibGold and Global Wakaf Al-Quran.

Before tech full-time, I supervised a retail team at Travel For All. It taught me leadership, scheduling and how to calm down an unhappy customer. Those skills still help every day.
